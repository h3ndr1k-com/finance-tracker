Exploration

From Hearth to Minimal

The 3.0 refresh drops the warm editorial palette for a neutral canvas with a single lime accent. A centered pill navigation and split list/detail panels replace the flat tab bar and wide transaction table, while the dollar-sign mark replaces the serif "L".

Motion & accessibility

Stable, fast, and legible

  • 160–220ms feedback with cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1).
  • Cards animate border and shadow only; controls may use transform and opacity.
  • Graph detail uses a fixed viewport, preventing hover-driven layout shift.
  • Every control has a visible 3px focus ring and keyboard activation.
  • Normal text targets at least 4.5:1; large text and non-text UI target 3:1.
  • prefers-reduced-motion removes decorative animation.
